02 / METHOD LESS NOISE. MORE SIGNAL.

You don’t need to quantify everything.

You need a small set of honest signals, recorded consistently enough to reveal the shape of your days.

  1. 01

    Choose your signals

    Create columns for what matters now—from sleep and movement to prayer, focus, mood, or a single daily note.

  2. 02

    Log without friction

    Click a cell, enter the value, move on. Typed fields keep each signal useful without slowing you down.

  3. 03

    Read the month

    See days together, spot the gaps, and use monthly summaries to notice patterns worth acting on.
